title: Weak Morality
---

Is it moral to do right in fear of consequence? Maybe morality is akin to bravery; you cannot be brave without fear just as you can't be moral without acceptance?

Whether that hints at the truth or not, it begs an important question: do I feel moral because I lack power? Can you be moral without the strength to affect the world?

I may not be helpless, but what if my agency transcended consequence? Does power itself make one less moral, or merely enable them to be moral?
